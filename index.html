About Ascovita

The manufacturer
behind the brand.

Ascovita Healthcare is a WHO-GMP certified, FSSAI approved nutraceutical manufacturer based in Anand, Gujarat — India's nutraceutical manufacturing hub. We are not a retail storefront; we are the formulation, production and quality engine behind our own consumer brands and behind the private label brands we manufacture for.

Mission — Make premium-grade nutrition manufacturing accessible and dependable for health brands, at any scale.

Vision — To be India's most trusted nutraceutical manufacturing partner for effervescent and specialty supplement formats, recognised for quality, compliance and export reliability.
