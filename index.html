Before we begin

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Ask our team →
Can you manufacture under my brand name?

Yes. Ascovita offers private-label and contract manufacturing for nutraceutical products. Share your category and packaging requirements so we can recommend a suitable formulation and manufacturing route.

Which product formats can I discuss?

Our range includes effervescent tablets, conventional tablets, capsules, softgels, powders and sachets. Ask our team to confirm availability and specifications for your product.

What information do you need for a quotation?

Share the product or ingredients, approximate quantity, pack size and target market. If you are starting with an idea, we can help you define the brief.

What is the minimum order quantity?

Selected products are available from 500 units per SKU. The minimum depends on formulation, format and packaging, and is confirmed in your quotation.

Do you work with international buyers?

Yes. We discuss private-label manufacturing, packaging and export documentation with international partners. Requirements are reviewed for the destination market before production.
