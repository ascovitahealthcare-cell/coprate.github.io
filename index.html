Our brands

Three routes
to market.

Ascovita Healthcare is the manufacturer. Everything we make reaches a customer through one of three brands, each built for a channel that behaves differently.

Channel policy · 2026

Why the prices differ between them.

A product on a chemist's shelf carries costs an online listing doesn't — distributor margin, retailer margin, stock credit, returns. When the same brand appears online cheaper, the retailer who stocked it loses, and eventually stops stocking it.

So we separated them. Ascovita is made for the trade and priced to protect it. Ascofizz is made for online, with its own SKUs and pack sizes. Same facility, same formulations team, same release testing — two identities, so neither channel undercuts the other.

Manufacturing

Effervescence is a
humidity problem.

An effervescent tablet is an acid and a base held apart by nothing but dryness. Everything about how we build a batch follows from that one fact.

What goes wrong when it's made badly

Let moisture in during granulation, compression or packing and the reaction starts early. The tablet swells, the strip bloats, and the customer opens a tube of powder. It usually happens weeks later, in a warehouse, long after anyone could catch it.

So the facility is built around controlling one variable: dehumidified production rooms held under 25% RH from granulation through to pack, moisture-barrier packaging, and a release test no batch skips.

Frequently asked questions

Answers,
before you ask.

The questions distributors, private-label clients and export buyers ask us most. Anything else, message us and a founder will reply.

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ starts at 500 units per SKU for private label and third-party manufacturing. Bulk and wholesale orders start at 500 units and step through volume tiers from there.

How long does a B2B order take?

First enquiry to first delivery is typically 3–4 weeks. You get a formulation proposal and pricing sheet within 48 hours, then lab samples for approval before production starts.

Do you manufacture to my own formulation?

Yes. Third-party manufacturing means we produce to your exact specification in our GMP-certified facility, across effervescent, capsule, tablet, softgel, powder and sachet formats.

What certifications do you hold?

The plant is FSSAI licensed and WHO-GMP compliant, with regular government audits. Every batch is independently third-party lab tested for purity, potency and safety, with results on file for every SKU.

Can you handle label design and artwork?

Yes. We provide FSSAI-compliant label printing, full custom artwork support, QR code and barcode integration, and dispatch-ready carton packing.

Do you export?

Yes, through Jayraf Export, our dedicated export-import division led by our Export Director. We have served 30+ countries and handle Certificate of Analysis, MSDS, FSSAI, phytosanitary and customs documentation end to end.

Why are Ascovita and Ascofizz priced differently?

They are separate brands for separate channels. Ascovita is made for the retail trade and priced to protect distributor and retailer margin. Ascofizz is our online-only brand with its own SKUs and pack sizes, so e-commerce pricing never undercuts a retailer's shelf.

What packaging formats do you offer?

Alu-Alu, blister packs, strip packaging, effervescent tubes (10s and 20s), bottles, jars, standup pouches and single-serve sachets.

Who will I deal with?

Our founders directly. Every B2B enquiry reaches the leadership team — no sales reps and no intermediaries.

Do you offer a franchise?

Yes. The franchise programme is open pan-India and includes marketing support, an established supply chain and the Ascovita brand. It suits pharma distributors and health stores.
