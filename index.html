Why Ascovita and Ascofizz are priced differently

A product on a chemist's shelf carries costs an online listing doesn't: distributor margin, retailer margin, stock credit, returns. When the same brand appears online at a lower price, the retailer who stocked it loses — and eventually stops stocking it.

So we separated the brands. Ascovita is made for the trade and priced to protect it. Ascofizz is made for online, with its own SKUs and pack sizes. Same facility, same formulations team, same quality release — two identities, so neither channel undercuts the other.

02 — Manufacturing

Effervescence is a
humidity problem.

An effervescent tablet is an acid and a base held apart by nothing but dryness. Let moisture in during granulation, compression or packing and the reaction starts early — the tablet swells, the strip bloats, and the customer opens a tube of powder.

Everything about how we build a batch follows from that. Dehumidified production rooms, moisture-barrier packing, and a release test that no batch skips.
