Packaging

Every format
a brand needs

Shelf-ready formats for nutraceuticals and pharmaceuticals — produced, filled and sealed in our own plant with FSSAI-compliant artwork support. Not sure which format fits your product? Our Consultation Studio team recommends the best pack for your category.

01

Tablets · Alu-Alu blister

High-barrier Alu-Alu (aluminium/ALU) blister packs — the standard for moisture-sensitive nutraceutical and pharmaceutical tablets.

02

Tablets · strip packaging

Cost-efficient strip packs for standard tablets — secure, lightweight and retail-ready.

03

Capsules

Capsule blisters and bottles with tamper-evident sealing — all capsule sizes from our in-house lines.

04

Sachets

Single-dose sachets for powders and granules — on-the-go formats with precise dosing.

05

Effervescent tubes

Our flagship format — branded, moisture-barrier tubes for effervescent tablets, our plant’s speciality.

06

Bottle packs

Look packs
