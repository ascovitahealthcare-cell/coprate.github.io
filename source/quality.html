Your procurement checklist

What to review
before production.

A shared brief helps your team and our manufacturing team make the same decisions.

01 · Credentials

Licences & scope

Ask for the applicable FSSAI licence and current certificates. Review the manufacturing entity, address, validity and the activities or products covered.

02 · Product

Agreed specifications

Confirm ingredients, amounts, flavour, format, pack size and the product’s intended market. Document what is included in the manufacturing scope.

03 · Quality

Testing & batch records

Discuss the applicable test parameters, certificate of analysis, batch identification and release documentation for your order.

04 · Packaging

Artwork & pack approval

Review the label, declarations, packaging material, batch coding and finished-pack specification before approving production.
