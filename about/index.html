About Ascovita
Healthcare.

A nutraceutical manufacturer and brand development partner in Anand, Gujarat — making premium nutrition accessible without compromising on quality or affordability.

Who we are

A plant, a formulations team,
a founding promise.

Ascovita Healthcare formulates, manufactures and delivers nutraceuticals — effervescent tablets, tablets, capsules, softgels, powders and sachets — from our own FSSAI licensed, WHO-GMP compliant facility in Anand. We manufacture for our own brands, private-label clients, clinics, distributors and international buyers.

We focus on B2B: manufacturing, product development, packaging and brand-launch support. Global opportunities are open to international partners who want a single accountable manufacturer in India.

Our story

India has the world's highest rate of micronutrient deficiency — yet most supplement brands charge a premium that keeps quality nutrition out of reach. We founded Ascovita to break that cycle.

Owning the plant is what makes it possible. There is no contract manufacturer in the middle taking a margin, and nobody else deciding what quality means on our behalf. The same standard applies whether the batch carries your brand, one of ours, or ships overseas.

“From formulation to manufacturing of nutraceutical products — one standard, no shortcuts.”

— The Ascovita Healthcare promise

Facility

Manufacturing environment

An effervescent tablet is an acid and a base held apart by nothing but dryness. Our rooms are held under 25% RH from granulation through to pack, with moisture-barrier packaging and a release test no batch skips.

FSSAI-licensed plant

Fully licensed food-grade manufacturing with regular government audits and full regulatory compliance.

All formats in-house

Effervescent tablets, capsules, softgels, tablets, powders and sachets — all under one roof, no outsourced steps.

Product development

Formulations developed and validated by certified nutritionists and food scientists.

Quality & compliance

If it isn't written down, it didn't happen.

Every batch we release carries a paper trail: which raw material lot went in, who compressed it, what the release tests said, and a retained sample on a shelf in case anyone ever needs to check. That record is what a distributor, a private-label client and an export buyer are all actually buying.
