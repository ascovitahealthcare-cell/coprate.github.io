The Ascovita launch method

Eight steps.
Zero gaps.

Follow how your brand moves from an idea to a business that is selling — with a dedicated Ascovita team member owning every step.

01

Market research

Our team studies your category, competitors and pricing so the brand launches with real market fit — not guesswork.

02

Product selection & formulation

Pick from our proven catalogue or co-develop custom formulations with certified nutritionists — actives, flavour, format and dose.

03

Brand identity & design

Logo, colour system, packaging design and label artwork — a complete identity built by our design team, not outsourced fragments.

04

Legal & compliance

FSSAI licensing, product registrations, label regulations, GST and documentation — handled so your brand is legal from day one.

05

Manufacturing

Produced in our own FSSAI licensed, WHO-GMP compliant plant — effervescent, tablets, capsules, softgels, powders and sachets.

06

Website & e-commerce

A full e-commerce website for your brand — storefront, payments, delivery integrations and marketplace onboarding, built by our web team.

07

Dedicated marketing team

A dedicated team plans your launch and growth — campaigns, content, social and performance marketing that keeps selling after launch.

08

Launch & distribution

Retail, wholesale, franchise or D2C — we scale your brand across channels with priority stock and ongoing supply.
